# Our glossary (Matthew 8 onward)

One Greek word, one English word. Every choice we make goes here, with where we first used it, so later units stay consistent. Tim's choices for 1–7 are in `translation-style.md` §3; this list picks up from there. Sounds-like spellings are for learning, stressed syllable in capitals. In the units, a repeated highlighted word shows its Greek (`word-original`) at every occurrence, in the form used there.
